Made for a second life

Nothing forgotten.
Nothing quite the same.

SpecterMirror brings older Linux laptops back as interactive haunted mirrors. A portable Rust engine will power both a CPU-only native installation and a local WebAssembly browser experience.

The foundation validates frames and renders a synthetic mirror through X11. Real webcam effects are next. The longer view is a modular Smart Mirror, with Halloween as its first filter theme.

Your reflection stays yours.

The finished mirror will process frames on your device, with camera access only after you choose it. No accounts, frame uploads or hidden recording.
