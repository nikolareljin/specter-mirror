Made for a second life

Nothing forgotten.
Nothing quite the same.

SpecterMirror brings older Linux laptops back as interactive haunted mirrors. A portable Rust engine powers a local WebAssembly browser demo and is designed for a CPU-only native installation.

The native foundation validates frames and renders a synthetic mirror through X11. The browser demo runs three processing modes on a webcam or a generated scene; native camera capture is still ahead. The longer view is a modular Smart Mirror, with Halloween as its first filter theme.

Your reflection stays yours.

The browser mirror processes frames on your device, with camera access only after you press Start camera. No accounts, frame uploads or hidden recording.
